Range accuracy: distance comes from how big the target looks (face width for people with a visible face, body height otherwise, typical size for pets). A browser cannot read a phone's LiDAR sensor, so this is camera-based ranging. Calibrate once on a target at a known distance for best results. Far targets get less accurate, and a heartbeat can only be read when a face is close and large in the picture.
